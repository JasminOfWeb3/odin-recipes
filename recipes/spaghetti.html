Spaghetti



Description

This is a classic spaghetti recipe that is easy to make and delicious!

Spaghetti is a traditional Italian dish made with pasta and a flavorful tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until browned.
  3. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Drain the cooked spaghetti noodles and add them to the skillet with the sauce.
  6. Toss everything together until well combined.
  7. Serve hot, topped with grated parmesan cheese.
