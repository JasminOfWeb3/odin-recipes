Egg Sauce



Description

This is a simple and tasty egg sauce recipe that can be served with rice, yam, or bread.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then peel and chop them.
  2. In a pan, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
  3. Add the chopped onions and sauté until translucent.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
  5. Add the chopped eggs to the pan and stir gently.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, then remove from heat.
